# Caregiver
[Back to this section](README.md) · [Home](../README.md)

## What is it?
The Caregiver is a brand archetype organized around protecting, supporting, and helping others. In Margaret Mark and Carol S. Pearson's brand-archetype framework, its central promise is compassionate service: make people feel supported and safer. It is a strategic storytelling model, not a clinical personality type or evidence that a company is caring.

## How do you recognize or use it?
Look for a patient, reassuring voice; clear explanations; welcoming, respectful imagery; and practical help that reduces someone's burden. It can suit health and family services, education, charities, and products built around care or safety. Make the support concrete, preserve the audience's agency, and avoid pity, guilt, overpromising, or presenting self-sacrifice as the caregiver's duty.

Example 1 uses FIRST DETOUR, a fictional Explorer brand and free neighborhood walking map. Example 2 uses Steady Hand, a fictional Caregiver service and free family-care planning guide. Both offers are hypothetical and created for this exercise.

### Example 1
![Swiss-inspired FIRST DETOUR hero with an adult woman holding a folded city map, the headline “Find a new way through your city.”, and a “Get the free map” button](../assets/tutorial/modern-photo.png)


Archetype: Explorer
Style: Swiss/International Typographic Style-inspired modernist layout.
Persuasion: Reciprocity, by offering a useful walking map for free.
Headline: Find a new way through your city.
CTA: **Get the free map** — the fictional offer requires no email.

The strict grid and restrained typography make the map offer easy to understand, while the woman looking toward the headline draws attention back to the copy. The free walking map applies reciprocity without inventing testimonials or pressuring the visitor.

### Example 2
![Memphis-inspired Steady Hand hero with two family members reviewing a care plan, the headline “Care for someone else. Get support for your next step.”, and a “Get the free care guide” button](../assets/heroes/caregiver/postmodernist.png)


Archetype: Caregiver
Style: [Memphis (postmodernist)](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/what-is-postmodernism)
Persuasion: Reciprocity, by offering the same practical guide for free.
Headline: Care for someone else. Get support for your next step.
CTA: **Get the free care guide** — opens the guide download.

The vivid, irregular Memphis-inspired shapes make the service feel approachable rather than clinical, while the caring scene and plain-language offer keep its purpose clear. A free planning guide makes the Caregiver promise tangible and applies reciprocity without inventing testimonials or pressuring the visitor.
