# Vaporwave
[Back to this section](README.md) · [Home](../README.md)

## What is it?
Vaporwave emerged online in the early 2010s as an internet-native art and music movement before becoming a visual design style in its own right. It draws heavily on 1980s and early-1990s consumer and corporate aesthetics — mall culture, early computer graphics, VHS tapes — and recontextualizes them with irony, nostalgia, and a sense of detachment from the commercial optimism those images originally sold.

## How do you recognize or use it?
Characteristics: pastel gradients (especially pink and cyan), Greco-Roman statuary, glitch effects, retro computer interfaces and grid horizons, and typography that mixes Japanese characters with 1980s-style sans-serif or chrome text.

It suits brands wanting to signal irony, nostalgia, or a deliberately "aesthetic" online-native sensibility — music, streetwear, and digital products aimed at younger, internet-fluent audiences use it often.
