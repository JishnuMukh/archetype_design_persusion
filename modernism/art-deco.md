# Art Deco

## What is it?
Art Deco emerged in the 1920s and 1930s, first visible at the 1925 Paris Exposition Internationale des Arts Décoratifs et Industriels Modernes, the event that gave the style its name. It developed as a reaction against the flowing, organic lines of Art Nouveau, favoring geometric precision, symmetry, and a sense of luxury tied to modern industry and new materials like chrome and glass. It reflected the optimism of the machine age: the belief that technology and craftsmanship could combine into something glamorous rather than cold.

## How do you recognize or use it?
Characteristics: bold geometric shapes (chevrons, sunbursts, stepped forms), strong symmetry, rich color paired with metallic accents (gold, chrome, black), and elegant, often stylized typography with sharp or rounded geometric letterforms.

It suits brands wanting to signal luxury, glamour, or a sense of confident, aspirational modernity — hospitality, fashion, and premium product packaging use it often.
